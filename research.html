RESEARCH & INNOVATION

Turning Communication Theory into Hardware

Developing wireless, optical, and high-speed digital communication systems from theoretical mathematical models and simulations to validated physical prototypes on FPGA and laboratory testbeds.

CORE RESEARCH INTERESTS

Key Research Areas

📡

Advanced Modulation Formats

Design and experimental evaluation of high-order modulation formats including 128-QAM, SC-FDM, and coherent optical OFDM for wireless and optical communications.

🧩

FPGA Prototyping & Real DSP

Realization of real-time communication transceiver prototypes using high-speed FPGA hardware pipelines, digital filtering, and hardware acceleration engines.

🌊

Underwater VLC & Optical Wireless

Visible Light Communication (VLC) systems for aquatic environmental monitoring, optical wireless sensor networks, and high-throughput point-to-point links.

⚙️

Low Phase Noise PLL Design

Design and optimization of low phase-noise phase-locked loops (PLL) for carrier recovery and frequency synthesis in wireless and optical transceivers.

🩺

Smart Optical Sensors & Healthcare

Photonic and optical sensor integration with microcontrollers (Arduino, ESP32) for patient monitoring, medical devices, and industrial automation.

🌐

Optical Network Architecture

Coherent optical transmission, dispersion compensation, and next-generation broadband optical network infrastructure analysis.
