COLLABORATION & ALLIANCES

Research Strengthened through Strategic Partnership

BONLAB actively collaborates with international space exploration missions, computer and electrical engineering departments, academic institutions, schools, and national research funding bodies.

Deep Space & Lunar Exploration Mission

National Astronomical Research Institute of Thailand (NARIT)

สถาบันวิจัยดาราศาสตร์แห่งชาติ (สดร.) · Thailand–China Chang'E-7 (CE-7) Lunar Exploration Mission & CE-7 MATCH instrument payload development.

CIOMP / Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS)

Changchun Institute of Optics, Fine Mechanics and Physics · Space radiation detection & FPGA communication architecture for lunar research (ILRS).

Academic & Computer Engineering Alliances

Department of Computer Engineering (มทร.อีสาน)

สาขาวิชาวิศวกรรมคอมพิวเตอร์ คณะวิศวกรรมศาสตร์ มทร.อีสาน ขอนแก่น · วิจัยร่วมด้าน Embedded Systems, Edge AI, FPGA Computing และสถาปัตยกรรมฮาร์ดแวร์ประมวลผลความเร็วสูง

RMUTI Khon Kaen Campus

Faculty of Engineering, Department of Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering · Home laboratory and national engineering research center.

Paderborn University (UPB)

Germany · Joint research on Coherent Optical OFDM, advanced optical modulation, and high-speed digital signal processing algorithms.

University of Yamanashi (Japan)

Faculty of Engineering · Collaborative research on Optical Wireless Communication (OWC), Visible Light Communication (VLC), and underwater optical links.

Mahanakorn University of Technology (MUT)

Academic network and collaborative seminars in advanced Photonics, satellite communication concepts, and space technology innovation.

Academic Consortiums & Professional Societies

EEAAT / EECON Network

Electrical Engineering Academic Association (Thailand) · Annual EECON Conference leadership, technical committees, and Best Paper Award recognition.

ECTI Association

Electrical Engineering/Electronics, Computer, Telecommunications and IT Association · General Chair & Technical Committee for ECTI-CON 2024.

9 RMUTs Engineering Network (EENET)

Academic sub-committee network across 9 Rajamangala Universities for curriculum standards and collaborative engineering research.

IEEE Thailand Section

IEEE Communications and Signal Processing Societies · Global technical standards, publication tracks, and professional academic exchange.

Youth Innovation & STEM Education Outreach

Youth Innovation Project

Judging committee and technical mentorship for student innovators developing AIoT, embedded systems, and smart electronic solutions.

Kham Sakaesaeng School

Science & Technology hands-on workshops for special classroom students (ISP & EIS) and pre-engineering university guidance.

BONLAB Hands-on Engineering Workshops

Specialized practical training in FPGA digital design, MATLAB/Simulink programming, Arduino microcontrollers, and IoT sensor circuit assembly.

Government & National Research Funding

National Research Council of Thailand (NRCT)

National research grants supporting high-impact telecommunication, wireless sensors, and digital innovation projects.

Thailand Science Research and Innovation (TSRI)

Strategic funding for frontier science, fundamental research, and technology commercialization frameworks.

PMU-B

Program Management Unit for Human Resources & Institutional Development, Research and Innovation in deep tech and space fields.

RMUTI Research Fund

Institutional research grants and laboratory capability development support at RMUTI Khon Kaen Campus.
