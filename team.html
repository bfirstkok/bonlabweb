The People Behind BONLAB

Assoc. Prof. Dr. Kidsanapong Puntsri

Lab Director & Founder · IEEE Senior Member · Dr.-Ing.

Head of BON LAB and Associate Professor in the Department of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering, RMUTI Khon Kaen Campus. He received his Dr.-Ing. degree from The University of Paderborn, Germany, specializing in optical communication systems, FPGA real-time DSP, and advanced modulation formats.
