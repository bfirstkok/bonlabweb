ABOUT BONLAB

Broadband Optical Network Laboratory

A dedicated research laboratory for wireless, optical, and digital communication systems — from mathematical signal design to working hardware prototypes on FPGA and optical testbeds.

LAB DIRECTOR & FOUNDER

Assoc. Prof. Dr. Kidsanapong Puntsri

รศ.ดร.กฤษณะพงศ์ พันธ์ศรี is an Associate Professor and Head of the Broadband Optical Network Laboratory (BON LAB) in the Department of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, Rajamangala University of Technology Isan (RMUTI), Khon Kaen Campus.

His primary research focuses on advanced optical communication systems, high-order modulation formats (such as 128-QAM SC-FDM), real-time DSP on FPGA, low phase-noise phase-locked loops (PLL), and Visible Light Communications (VLC).

He has served as an active reviewer for top-tier international journals including IEEE Transactions on Signal Processing, IEEE Communications Letters, Elsevier Optical Fiber Technology, and IEEE Photonics Technology Letters.
