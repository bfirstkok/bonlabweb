128-QAM SC-FDM Optical Wireless System

Real-time digital signal processing, high-order constellation mapping, and hardware verification for high-speed optical communications.

Project Overview

At BONLAB, we investigate the realization of Single-Carrier Frequency Division Multiplexing (SC-FDM) combined with high-order Quadrature Amplitude Modulation (128-QAM) for optical wireless links. SC-FDM provides lower Peak-to-Average Power Ratio (PAPR) compared to traditional OFDM, making it exceptionally well-suited for optical emitters with nonlinear transfer characteristics.

Our team implements real-time baseband DSP blocks directly into FPGA hardware, validating theoretical error-vector magnitude (EVM) and bit error rate (BER) through physical optical channels.

Key Innovations & Technical Highlights

  • High Spectral Efficiency: 128-QAM modulation constellation design providing dense information throughput.
  • Low PAPR Performance: SC-FDM discrete Fourier transform (DFT) precoding to prevent laser diode saturation.
  • FPGA Real-Time DSP: Pipelined digital filtering, equalization, and symbol synchronization implemented on programmable digital hardware.
  • Low Phase-Noise PLL: Customized phase-locked loop synchronization reducing phase jitter in optical carrier recovery.

Laboratory Verification & Testbeds

Experiments were conducted in the BONLAB facilities at RMUTI Khon Kaen Campus using high-bandwidth arbitrary waveform generators, mixed-signal oscilloscopes, semiconductor laser modules, and high-speed photodetectors.
