128-QAM SC-FDM Optical Wireless Communication System

Executive Summary & System Architecture

At BONLAB, we investigate and demonstrate the hardware realization of Single-Carrier Frequency Division Multiplexing (SC-FDM) combined with high-order 128-QAM constellation mapping for high-speed optical wireless channels. Traditional OFDM suffers from severe Peak-to-Average Power Ratio (PAPR), causing nonlinear clipping and distortion in semiconductor laser diodes and high-power LEDs. By incorporating Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT) precoding, SC-FDM drastically suppresses PAPR while preserving the multipath mitigation benefits of multicarrier systems.

Our team designed and verified the entire physical layer transceivers on FPGA hardware pipelines, establishing seamless constellation demodulation, low error-vector magnitude (EVM), and robust transmission over atmospheric and optical wireless testbeds.

FPGA & REAL-TIME HARDWARE PROTOTYPING

Real-time FPGA DSP Transceiver & AWGN Hardware Engine

Executive Summary & Architecture

BONLAB specializes in translating theoretical digital communications into real-time programmable hardware logic (FPGA). We developed pipelined hardware modules for 16-QAM and 64-QAM transceivers, including a reconfigurable Additive White Gaussian Noise (AWGN) channel emulator based on the Box-Muller method combined with CORDIC square root calculation engines.

This hardware testbed allows direct, real-time Bit Error Rate (BER) characterization entirely inside FPGA silicon at clock rates exceeding 200 MHz, without relying on slow software-based computer simulations.

Hardware Capabilities & Key Features

🎲

Box-Muller CORDIC AWGN

Hardware-efficient generation of true Gaussian noise distributions for high-speed channel emulation.

⚡

High-throughput parallel architecture for real-time multicarrier modulation and demodulation.

📊

Automated BER Measurement

Real-time hardware bit error counter measuring millions of transmitted bits per second.

VISIBLE LIGHT COMMUNICATION · AQUATIC MONITORING

Underwater VLC Monitoring & Internet of Underwater Things (IoUT)

Executive Summary & Underwater Testbed

Standard radio frequency (RF) signals suffer extreme attenuation in water, making high-speed underwater communication a major technological challenge. BONLAB developed Visible Light Communication (VLC) optical transceivers using blue-green semiconductor optical emitters operating in the minimum attenuation optical window of clear and turbid water.

We constructed a dedicated laboratory water tank testbed equipped with optical transmitters, underwater sensor probes, and digital receiver circuits to validate telemetry for aquatic environmental monitoring and IoUT sensor nodes.

SMART HEALTHCARE · NATIONAL SILVER MEDAL

Smart Photonic Vital-Sign IoT Monitor

Executive Summary & Applied Health Technology

Awarded the Silver Medal at the Thailand Research Expo (มหกรรมงานวิจัยแห่งชาติ), this project applies microcontroller systems and optical photonic sensors to capture and process vital signs (heart rate, SpO2, body temperature) in real time.

Mentored by Assoc. Prof. Dr. Kidsanapong Puntsri, student engineers developed a complete hardware unit with cloud IoT telemetry, enabling healthcare staff to monitor patient telemetry remotely with high precision.
